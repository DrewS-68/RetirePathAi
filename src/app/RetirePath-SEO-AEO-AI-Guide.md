WHAT IS AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

SEO vs AEO Comparison:

SEO (Traditional) | AEO (New AI Era)
Optimizing for Google Search | Optimizing for AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)
Goal: Rank on Page 1 | Goal: Be THE answer AI cites
Users click through to your site | AI answers the question directly (may not click)
Focuses on keywords | Focuses on comprehensive, authoritative answers
10 blue links | 1 AI-generated answer with sources

Example Search:

SEO Approach:
- User searches: "best retirement villages Melbourne"
- Google shows 10 website links
- User clicks on #1 result (your site)
- You get traffic

AEO Approach:
- User asks ChatGPT: "What are the best retirement villages in Melbourne?"
- ChatGPT generates answer with 5 village recommendations
- IF optimized: ChatGPT cites RetirePath.com as source
- IF NOT optimized: ChatGPT uses competitor data, you get nothing
