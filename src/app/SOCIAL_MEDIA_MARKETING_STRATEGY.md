RetirePath Social Media Marketing Strategy


BEST PLATFORMS FOR YOUR AUDIENCE

Primary Platforms:

1. Facebook (TOP PRIORITY)
- 70%+ of 65+ Australians use Facebook
- Best for retirement-age audience
- Great for community building, testimonials, and educational content
- Facebook Groups for retirees considering village living

2. LinkedIn
- Target adult children (decision influencers)
- B2B for village operators
- Thought leadership content

3. YouTube
- Village tours, expert interviews, educational content
- High engagement for this demographic
- SEO benefits

Secondary Platforms:
4. Instagram - Adult children & family members who influence decisions
5. Pinterest - Downsizing tips, retirement lifestyle inspiration


AI-POWERED CONTENT STRATEGY

YES, most can be AI-assisted!

Content Types You Can Generate with AI:

1. Educational Posts
- "5 Red Flags in Retirement Village Contracts"
- "Understanding Entry Fees vs. Monthly Costs"
- "Downsizing Timeline: 6 Months Before Your Move"

2. SEO Blog Posts
- Village reviews and comparisons
- State-by-state guides
- Contract analysis tips

3. Video Scripts
- ChatGPT/Claude for scripts
- Tools like Descript, Pictory.ai, or Synthesia for video creation

4. Social Media Captions
- Daily tips, statistics, inspiration
- User testimonial formatting

5. Ad Copy
- Facebook/Google ad variations
- A/B testing different angles


AI TOOLS TO USE

Content Creation:
- ChatGPT/Claude - Blog posts, captions, scripts
- Midjourney/DALL-E - Graphics (though real village photos are better)
- Canva AI - Social media templates with AI writing
- Jasper.ai - Marketing copy specifically

Video:
- Pictory.ai - Turn blog posts into videos
- Descript - Video editing with AI
- ElevenLabs - AI voiceovers

Scheduling & Management:
- Buffer/Hootsuite - Schedule posts across platforms
- Later - Visual planning for Instagram
- Metricool - Analytics across all platforms

AI Social Media Managers:
- Predis.ai - Creates social posts from URLs
- Simplified - All-in-one AI marketing platform


RECOMMENDED CONTENT CALENDAR

Facebook (Daily):
- Monday: Educational tip
- Tuesday: Village spotlight
- Wednesday: User testimonial/success story
- Thursday: Contract/legal tip
- Friday: Weekend inspiration
- Weekend: Lifestyle content

LinkedIn (3x/week):
- Industry insights
- Village operator features
- B2B partnership opportunities

YouTube (2x/month):
- Expert interviews
- Village tours
- How-to guides


WHAT AI CAN'T DO WELL (Needs Human Touch)

1. Authentic testimonials - Real user stories
2. Community management - Responding to comments/DMs
3. Village photography - Real photos > AI images
4. Relationship building - Genuine engagement
5. Crisis management - Sensitive issues need human judgment


SPECIFIC CAMPAIGN IDEAS

1. "Know Before You Sign" Series
- Weekly contract tips
- AI-generated infographics
- Drive traffic to your Contract Review tool

2. Village Spotlight Thursdays
- Feature villages from your database
- Tag operators (builds relationships)
- User engagement through polls

3. Family Decision Series
- Target adult children on LinkedIn/Instagram
- "How to Help Your Parents Choose"
- Promote Family Guide feature

4. Success Stories
- Video testimonials (can use AI for editing)
- Before/after decluttering journeys


METRICS TO TRACK

- Engagement rate (not just followers)
- Click-through to website
- Free trial signups
- Conversion to paid tiers
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)


QUICK START ACTION PLAN

Week 1:
1. Set up business accounts on Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube
2. Use ChatGPT to generate 30 days of educational posts
3. Create 5 Canva templates for consistent branding
4. Schedule first 2 weeks with Buffer

Week 2-4:
1. Start Facebook Group "Retirement Village Decisions Made Easy"
2. Publish first YouTube video
3. Launch Facebook ads targeting 60-75 age group in Australia
4. Engage with comments daily (human touch!)


BUDGET ALLOCATION

- $500-1000/month for Facebook/Instagram ads
- $50-200/month for AI tools (Jasper, Pictory, Canva Pro)
- $0-100/month for scheduling tools
- Time investment: 5-10 hours/week initially


BOTTOM LINE

You can automate 60-70% of content creation with AI, but you'll need human oversight for:
- Strategy
- Community management
- Authenticity and genuine engagement

Start with Facebook and LinkedIn, use AI for content generation, but keep the human connection in engagement!


ADDITIONAL NOTES

- Focus on educational value first, sales second
- Build trust through transparency (contract analysis, honest reviews)
- Leverage your unique database of 2,500+ villages
- Partner with village operators for co-marketing opportunities
- Consider influencer partnerships (retirement lifestyle bloggers)
- Run Facebook Live Q&A sessions monthly
- Create downloadable guides as lead magnets
