# RetirePath - Social Media Strategy & Setup Guide

## Overview
This guide provides recommendations for setting up and managing social media accounts for RetirePath, a platform helping retirees transition to retirement villages. The strategy focuses on reaching both retirees (65+) and their adult children (35-55) who often help with the decision-making process.

---

## PART 1: PLATFORM PRIORITIES

### Your Target Audiences:

**Primary Audience:** Retirees (65-80 years old)
- Active on Facebook
- Growing presence on YouTube
- Some use LinkedIn
- Limited on Instagram/TikTok

**Secondary Audience:** Adult Children (35-55 years old)
- Active on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn
- Use YouTube for research
- Some on TikTok
- Professional networks

**Tertiary Audience:** Retirement Village Operators
- Active on LinkedIn
- Professional Facebook groups
- Industry forums

   - About: Full description with keywords
   - Website: Link to RetirePath.com
   - Contact: Business email and phone

3. **Add Details:**
   - Products/Services: Premium, Family memberships
   - Price range: $$
   - Hours: Business hours or "Always Open" for platform
   - Location: Business address (if physical)

4. **Set Up Call-to-Actions:**

   - @RetirePath or @RetirePathAU

6. **Privacy & Settings:**

## PART 9: COMPLIANCE & LEGAL

### Important Considerations:

**Testimonials & Reviews:**
- Always get written permission
- Avoid health/medical claims
- Don't promise specific outcomes
- Include disclaimers if needed

**Image Rights:**
- Never use photos without permission
- Get model releases for people's faces
- Credit photographers when required
- Use only licensed stock photos

**Financial Advice:**
- Include disclaimer: "Not financial advice"
- Recommend consulting professionals
- Don't make guarantees about savings

**Retirement Village Listings:**
- Only use authorized village photos
- Verify all information before posting
- Update prices/availability regularly
- Clear labeling of paid partnerships

**Privacy:**
- Never share user data publicly
- Blur/remove identifying details in screenshots
- GDPR/Privacy Act compliance
- Terms of service for social media use

**Copyright:**
- Don't use copyrighted music without license
- Instagram/Facebook music library is safe
- YouTube: Use YouTube Audio Library
- Attribute quotes and sources

**Accessibility:**
- Add alt text to images (screen readers)
- Captions on all videos
- High contrast text
- Large, readable fonts

---

### Social Media Policy (For Team):

**Guidelines:**
1. Always respond professionally
2. Never argue with commenters
3. Escalate complaints to management
4. Protect user privacy always
5. Fact-check before posting
6. Disclose partnerships (#ad, #sponsored)
7. Don't delete negative comments (unless abusive)
8. Keep politics and religion separate

## PART 11: CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### Potential Issues & Responses:

**Negative Review of Platform:**
1. Respond publicly and professionally
2. Apologize for poor experience
3. Offer to resolve privately via DM/email
4. Follow up and fix the issue
5. Ask if they'd update review after resolution

**Complaint About Featured Village:**
1. Take seriously - this is critical
2. Investigate immediately
3. Contact village operator
4. May need to remove listing
5. Update review system

**Misinformation in Comments:**
1. Politely correct with facts
2. Provide source/link
3. Don't be defensive
4. Educate respectfully

**Spam/Scam in Comments:**
1. Delete immediately
2. Ban user
3. Report to platform
4. Warn community if widespread

**Data Breach/Security Issue:**
1. Immediately take platforms dark (pause posts)
2. Post official statement
3. Direct users to email for updates
4. Follow legal requirements for notification
5. Update hourly until resolved
