## 🕐 THE SEO TIMELINE PROBLEM

### **How Long SEO Actually Takes:**

| Action | Time Required | Can't Rush This |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Google discovers new site | 1-2 weeks | ⏰ |
| Google indexes pages | 2-4 weeks | ⏰ |
| Content "ages" (ranking factor) | 3-6 months | ⏰ |
| Rank on Page 1 (competitive) | 6-12 months | ⏰ |
| Build domain authority | 12-24 months | ⏰ |

**Key Insight:** SEO is the ONLY marketing channel you can't rush with money.
- Ads? Instant traffic (if you pay)
- Email? Instant if you have a list
- Social? Immediate if you have followers
- **SEO? REQUIRES TIME** ⏰

**Solution:** Start the clock NOW, not at launch.

### **On-Page SEO Template:**

**Every Page Must Have:**

1. **Unique Title Tag** (50-60 characters)
   - Format: `Primary Keyword | RetirePath`
   - Example: `Retirement Villages in Sydney | RetirePath`

2. **Unique Meta Description** (150-160 characters)
   - Include keyword + call-to-action
   - Example: `Find and compare 487 retirement villages in Sydney. Read reviews, check pricing, and book tours. Free tool by RetirePath.`

3. **H1 Tag** (One per page, includes primary keyword)
   - Example: `Retirement Villages in Sydney: Compare 487 Options`

4. **H2-H6 Subheadings** (Include related keywords)
   - Use naturally in content structure

5. **URL Structure** (Clean, keyword-rich)
   - Good: `/villages/sydney/village-name`
   - Bad: `/page?id=12345`

6. **Image Alt Text** (Describe image + keyword when natural)
   - Example: `Retirement village gardens in Sydney NSW`

7. **Internal Links** (3-5 per page)
   - Link to related villages, blog posts, tools

8. **External Links** (1-2 authoritative sources)
   - Link to government sites, industry associations

9. **Word Count**
   - Homepage: 500-700 words
   - Village pages: 300-500 words
   - Blog posts: 1,500-3,000 words
   - Location pages: 800-1,200 words

## 🤖 AEO (AI ENGINE OPTIMIZATION)

### **What is AEO?**

**AEO = Optimizing for AI search engines like:**
- ChatGPT
- Google Gemini (Bard)
- Perplexity AI
- Microsoft Copilot

**Why it matters:**
- 40% of Gen Z uses ChatGPT instead of Google
- AI engines give ONE answer (not 10 links)
- Being THE answer = 100% of traffic
- Early movers win (like early SEO adopters in 2000s)

---

### **AEO vs SEO:**

| Factor | SEO | AEO |
|--------|-----|-----|
| **Goal** | Rank in top 10 | Be THE answer cited |
| **Format** | Keywords | Natural language |
| **Links** | Backlinks matter | Authority matters |
| **Content** | 1,500+ words | Concise + comprehensive |
| **Optimization** | Title tags, meta | Structured data, FAQs |

---

### **AEO Optimization Checklist:**

#### **1. Structured Data (Critical!)**

AI engines LOVE structured data. It's machine-readable.

**Implement:**
- [ ] LocalBusiness schema on all village pages
- [ ] FAQ schema on blog posts
- [ ] How-to schema on guides
- [ ] Review schema on testimonials
- [ ] Breadcrumb schema on all pages

**Example FAQ Schema:**
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What is a deferred management fee?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "A deferred management fee (DMF) is a percentage of the sale price paid when you leave a retirement village. Typically 25-35%, it's calculated based on how long you lived there."
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

---

#### **2. Natural Language Optimization**

People ask AI questions naturally:
- "What's the best retirement village in Sydney?"
- "How much does a retirement village cost?"
- "What are red flags in retirement village contracts?"

**Your content should:**
- Answer these questions directly
- Use question headings (H2, H3)
- Provide clear, concise answers
- Include examples

**Example:**
```markdown
## What is a Deferred Management Fee (DMF)?

A deferred management fee (DMF) is a percentage of your entry fee 
that's paid to the retirement village operator when you leave or sell 
your unit. 

Typically 25-35% of the sale price, the DMF is calculated based on:
- How long you lived in the village
- The sale price of your unit
- The terms in your contract

For example, if you paid a $500,000 entry fee and the DMF is 30%, 
you'd pay $150,000 when you leave.
```

^ AI engines can extract this cleanly and cite you.

---

#### **3. Featured Snippet Optimization**

Featured snippets are what AI engines pull from Google.

**Optimize for snippets:**

**Lists:**
```markdown
## 10 Red Flags in Retirement Village Contracts

1. **Exit fees over 35%** - Anything above 35% DMF is excessive
2. **No buyback guarantee** - You need protection if unit doesn't sell
3. **Limited refund period** - Look for at least 90 days cooling off
...
```

**Tables:**
```markdown
| Village Type | Entry Fee | Weekly Fee | Care Level |
|-------------|-----------|------------|------------|
| Independent Living | $300k-800k | $100-300 | None |
| Serviced Apartments | $400k-1M | $200-500 | Low-medium |
| Aged Care | $0-500k | $300-800 | High |
```

**Paragraphs:**
```markdown
## How Much Does a Retirement Village Cost?

Retirement villages in Australia typically cost between $300,000 
and $1 million for the entry fee, plus $100-$800 per week in 
ongoing charges. The total depends on location, village type, and 
care level provided.
```

---

#### **4. Authority Signals**

AI engines prioritize authoritative sources.

**Build authority:**
- [ ] Author bios (expertise)
- [ ] Citations from trusted sources
- [ ] External links to government sites
- [ ] Industry partnerships
- [ ] Media mentions
- [ ] Expert reviews

**Example:**
```markdown
## About the Author

This guide was written by RetirePath's research team, including 
Jane Smith (20+ years in aged care) and John Doe (retirement 
village contracts specialist). Reviewed by the Australian Retirement 
Village Association (ARVA).

Sources:
- Australian Government Aged Care Guide
- Retirement Villages Act 1999 (NSW)
- Consumer Affairs Victoria
```

---

#### **5. Entity Optimization**

AI engines understand ENTITIES (people, places, organizations).

**Help AI understand your entities:**

**Organization:**
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "RetirePath",
  "url": "https://retirepath.com",
  "logo": "https://retirepath.com/logo.png",
  "description": "Australia's leading retirement village comparison platform",
  "foundingDate": "2026",
  "founder": {
    "@type": "Person",
    "name": "Your Name"
  }
}
```

**Mention entities in content:**
- "According to the Australian Government's Aged Care website..."
- "As reported by ABC News..."
- "Data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics shows..."
