RetirePath Launch Cost Guide
Comprehensive External Costs for Website Launch
Prepared: January 2026


LEGAL & COMPLIANCE ($8,000 - $25,000)

Essential Legal:
- Terms of Service & Privacy Policy - $2,000-5,000 (lawyer review)
- Contract Review Disclaimer - $1,000-3,000 (critical since you're analyzing legal contracts)
- GDPR/Privacy Act Compliance - $1,500-3,000 (data handling, user rights)
- Business Structure Setup (PTY LTD) - $800-1,500
- Trademark Registration (RetirePath) - $250-500 per class

Aged Care Specific:
- Legal Review of "Contract Scoring" Feature - $2,000-5,000
  WARNING: CRITICAL - You're providing risk analysis on legal contracts - need lawyer to review disclaimers
  Must clearly state "not legal advice"
- Consumer Advice Licensing Review - $1,000-2,000
  Check if you need financial services license (unlikely but verify)


INSURANCE ($3,000 - $8,000/year)

- Professional Indemnity Insurance - $2,000-5,000/year
  Essential for advice/analysis platforms
- Public Liability Insurance - $500-1,000/year
- Cyber Liability Insurance - $1,500-3,000/year
  Covers data breaches (you're storing sensitive user data)


ACCOUNTING & ADMIN ($3,000 - $6,000/year)

- Accountant/Bookkeeper - $150-300/month ($1,800-3,600/year)
- Xero/MYOB Subscription - $50-70/month ($600-840/year)
- ABN/GST Registration - Free
- ASIC Annual Review - $273/year
- Tax Planning/BAS Lodgement - $500-1,500/year


PAYMENT PROCESSING (Ongoing)

- Stripe Fees - 1.75% + 30¢ per transaction (Australian cards)
  If selling $10k/month = ~$200/month in fees
- Stripe Atlas (if incorporating in US) - $500 one-time


TECH INFRASTRUCTURE ($200 - $600/month)

Current Stack:
- Supabase - $25-100/month (Pro plan for production)
- ScraperAPI - $49-249/month (depends on usage)
- Resend Email - $20-100/month (depends on volume)
- Domain & Hosting - $15-30/year (domain), Supabase handles hosting

Additional Subscriptions:
- Google Maps API - $200-500/month (you have interactive maps)
  First $200/month is free credit
  You're displaying maps for 2,569 villages - could add up!
- Error Monitoring (Sentry) - $26-80/month
- Uptime Monitoring (Pingdom/UptimeRobot) - $10-30/month


MARKETING & SEO ($2,000 - $10,000/month)

SEO & Content:
- SEO Tools (Ahrefs/SEMrush) - $99-399/month
- Content Writer (blog posts) - $150-500 per article
- SEO Agency (optional) - $1,500-5,000/month

Paid Advertising:
- Google Ads - $1,000-5,000/month
  High-value keywords: "retirement villages melbourne" = $5-15 CPC
  Your target market (seniors transitioning) has high LTV
- Facebook/Instagram Ads - $500-2,000/month
- Landing Page Optimization - $2,000-5,000 one-time

Organic Growth:
- Partnerships with Aged Care Advisors - Free/commission-based
- PR/Media Outreach - $1,500-3,000/month (agency) OR DIY free
- Email Marketing (Mailchimp/ConvertKit) - $20-100/month


CUSTOMER SUPPORT TOOLS ($100 - $300/month)

- Intercom/Crisp Chat - $39-99/month
- Help Desk (Zendesk/Freshdesk) - $15-79/month/agent
- Knowledge Base (Notion/GitBook) - $10-30/month


ADDITIONAL SUBSCRIPTIONS

- Design Tools (Figma Pro) - $12-15/month
- Project Management (Notion/Asana) - $10-25/month/user
- Password Manager (1Password) - $8/month
- Slack/Communication - Free-$8/month/user

CRITICAL PRIORITIES

1. Legal Review of Contract Analysis - Do this FIRST
   - You're scoring retirement village contracts for risk
   - One lawsuit could bankrupt the business

2. Professional Indemnity Insurance - Essential before launch

3. Privacy Policy & Terms - Required for Stripe, Supabase, GDPR

4. Accounting Setup - Track revenue/expenses from day 1 for tax


COST-SAVING STRATEGIES

Legal Templates - Use LawPath ($139/month) instead of lawyers for basic docs
DIY SEO - Learn SEO yourself initially (free courses)
Organic Marketing - Focus on content, partnerships before paid ads
Freemium Tools - Many SaaS tools have free tiers
Virtual Assistant - Hire Filipino VA for $5-10/hour for admin work
Government Grants - Check for startup/tech grants in your state


RECOMMENDED ACTION PLAN

Phase 1 - Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-4):
- Engage lawyer for contract review disclaimer ($2,000-5,000)
- Set up PTY LTD structure ($800-1,500)
- Purchase Professional Indemnity Insurance ($2,000-5,000)
- Create Terms of Service & Privacy Policy ($2,000-5,000)
- Set up Xero/accounting system ($50-70/month)
- Register trademark ($250-500)

Total: $7,000-12,000

Phase 2 - Launch (Weeks 5-8):
- Upgrade Supabase to Pro ($25-100/month)
- Set up error monitoring ($26-80/month)
- Implement live chat ($39-99/month)
- Start content marketing (DIY or $500-2,000/month)
- Set up Google Analytics & Search Console (Free)

Monthly: $500-2,500

Phase 3 - Growth (Month 3+):
- Invest in SEO tools ($99-399/month)
- Start paid advertising ($1,000-5,000/month)
- Hire content writer ($150-500/article)
- Consider PR agency ($1,500-3,000/month)

Monthly: $3,000-10,000
