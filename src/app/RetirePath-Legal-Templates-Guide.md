HOW LEGAL TEMPLATES WORK

Legal template services provide pre-written, lawyer-reviewed documents that you can customize for your business. Think of them as "fill-in-the-blank" legal documents.

The Process:
1. Choose your document type (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, etc.)
2. Answer questions about your business via online questionnaire
3. AI/System generates customized document based on your answers
4. Review and download the final document
5. Optional lawyer review (extra cost) for peace of mind

Cost Comparison:
- Custom Lawyer: $2,000-5,000 per document
- Template Service: $50-500 per document
- Savings: 80-90% cheaper

DOCUMENTS YOU NEED FOR RETIREPATH

Essential (Do First):

1. Privacy Policy ($99-299)
- Required by law if collecting personal data
- Must comply with Australian Privacy Act 1988
- GDPR compliance (if EU users access site)
- Template Service: LawPath or Legal123

2. Terms of Service ($99-299)
- User rights and obligations
- Payment terms (Stripe subscriptions)
- Account termination clauses
- Intellectual property ownership
- Template Service: LawPath or Legal123

3. Website Disclaimer ($99-199)
- "Not legal/financial advice"
- Limitation of liability
- CRITICAL for RetirePath - you're analyzing contracts
- Template Service: LawPath BUT recommend custom lawyer review for Contract Scoring feature

4. Refund Policy ($50-150)
- Required by Stripe
- Australian Consumer Law compliance
- Template Service: LawPath or Legal123


Important (Do Soon):

5. Subscription Agreement ($200-500)
- Terms for your 3 membership tiers
- Recurring billing terms
- Upgrade/downgrade policies
- Template Service: LawPath or Rocket Lawyer

6. Acceptable Use Policy ($99-199)
- What users can/can't do on platform
- Prevents abuse
- Template Service: LawPath

7. Cookie Policy ($50-150)
- Required if using cookies/analytics
- Template Service: LawPath or free generator


Optional (Nice to Have):

8. End User License Agreement (EULA) ($150-300)

10. API Terms of Use ($150-300) - if you offer API access


WHEN TEMPLATES AREN'T ENOUGH

You NEED a Custom Lawyer If:

1. Contract Scoring/Risk Analysis Feature
- You're providing analysis of legal retirement village contracts
- This is HIGH RISK - users might rely on your scoring to make $500k+ decisions
- One lawsuit = business bankruptcy
- Cost: $2,000-5,000 for custom disclaimer + review
- Recommended Firms:
  - Lawler Partners (tech specialist)
  - HWL Ebsworth (aged care specialist)
  - Holding Redlich (consumer law)

2. Financial Advice Licensing
- Your "Home Valuation" feature might trigger financial services regulations
- Need lawyer to confirm you DON'T need AFSL (Australian Financial Services License)
- Cost: $1,000-2,000 for opinion letter

3. Data Breach Scenarios
- Storing sensitive user data (contracts, financial info)
- Lawyer should review data handling procedures
- Cost: $1,500-3,000

KEY QUESTIONS TO ASK LAWYERS

When you contact lawyers, ask:

1. "Do you have experience with SaaS platforms or consumer advice websites?"
   - Want: Yes to either

2. "We provide risk scoring on retirement village contracts - is this legal advice?"
   - Want: Clear answer on regulatory risk

3. "What disclaimers do we need to avoid liability?"
   - Want: Specific wording recommendations

4. "Do we need an Australian Financial Services License (AFSL)?"
   - Want: Clear yes/no with reasoning

5. "Can you provide fixed-fee pricing?"
   - Want: $2,000-5,000 range (avoid hourly if possible)

6. "What's your turnaround time?"
   - Want: 1-2 weeks max
