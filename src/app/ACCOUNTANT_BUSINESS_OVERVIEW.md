RetirePath - Business Overview & Revenue Model

Prepared for Accountant Meeting
Date: December 3, 2024


Executive Summary

RetirePath is a comprehensive SaaS platform designed to help Australian retirees navigate the complex transition to retirement village living. The platform combines AI-powered tools, data analysis, and marketplace features to simplify what is typically one of the most stressful and confusing decisions retirees face.

Target Market: Australians aged 65+ transitioning to retirement villages (300,000+ potential users annually)


What RetirePath Does

Core Platform Features

RetirePath provides 7 main components that guide retirees through the entire transition process:

1. Village Matcher - AI-powered matching algorithm that recommends suitable retirement villages based on budget, location, lifestyle preferences, and care needs

2. Contract Review & Risk Scoring - Analyzes retirement village contracts and provides risk scores for:
   - Deferred Management Fees (DMF) - the percentage taken when you leave
   - Exit conditions and clauses
   - Ongoing fee structures
   - Inheritance impact for family members

3. Home Valuation - AI-powered property valuation tool to help retirees understand their current home's value and selling costs

4. Progress Tracker - Step-by-step timeline for decluttering, selling home, and transitioning to village living

5. Resources Library - Educational content about retirement villages, legal considerations, and financial planning

6. Family Guide - Information for adult children helping parents make this transition

7. Retirement Village Directory - Searchable database of retirement villages across Australia

Key Differentiators

- Supports all 4 retirement village models with different DMF structures (fixed, variable, capped, hybrid)
- Inheritance impact calculations - shows families exactly how much money will be left after DMF fees
- Contract risk analysis - simplifies complex legal documents into plain English
- End-to-end solution - from initial research to moving day

3. Agent Referral Program (Tertiary)

Real Estate Commission Revenue:

RetirePath connects retirees who need to sell their homes with trusted real estate agents. We earn referral commissions on successful sales.

Commission Structure:
- Standard real estate commission: 2-2.5% of sale price
- RetirePath referral fee: 20-30% of agent commission
- Average home value: $750,000
- Average commission earned per referral: $3,750 - $5,625

Revenue Potential:
- Target: 100 referrals per year
- Conversion rate: 60% (60 successful sales)
- Annual Revenue: $225,000 - $337,500
