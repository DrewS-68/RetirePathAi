# RETIREMENT VILLAGE RESIDENCE CONTRACT

**SUNNYVALE RETIREMENT VILLAGE**
123 Peaceful Lane, Brisbane QLD 4000

## PARTIES

**Operator:** Sunnyvale Retirement Living Pty Ltd (ABN 12 345 678 901)

**Resident:** [Your Name Here]

**Date of Contract:** 15 January 2024

---

## 1. CONTRACT TYPE

This is a **Loan/License Agreement** for the occupation of Unit 47, Sunnyvale Retirement Village.

---

## 2. ENTRY COSTS

### 2.1 Ingoing Contribution
The Resident agrees to pay an **Ingoing Contribution** of **$650,000** for the right to occupy the unit.

### 2.2 Payment Terms
- Deposit: $65,000 (10%) due within 14 days of contract signing
- Balance: $585,000 due on settlement (60 days from contract date)

### 2.3 Stamp Duty
The Resident is responsible for all stamp duty costs (if applicable in your state).

---

## 3. DEFERRED MANAGEMENT FEE (DMF)

### 3.1 DMF Structure
Upon departure from the village, the Operator will deduct a **Deferred Management Fee** calculated as follows:

- **Years 1-5:** 6% per year of the Ingoing Contribution
- **Years 6+:** 0% per year (capped at 30%)

**Example Calculation:**
- If you leave after 3 years: DMF = 18% of $650,000 = **$117,000**
- If you leave after 5 years: DMF = 30% of $650,000 = **$195,000**
- If you leave after 10 years: DMF = 30% of $650,000 = **$195,000** (capped)

### 3.2 When DMF is Payable
The DMF is deducted from your exit payment when:
- You permanently vacate the unit, OR
- The unit is resold to a new resident, OR
- You pass away and the estate settles

---

## 4. ONGOING COSTS

### 4.1 General Services Charge
**$650 per month** (reviewed annually, increases typically 3-5% per year)

This covers:
- Building insurance
- Common area maintenance
- Village management
- Gardening of common areas
- Security services
- Access to community facilities

### 4.2 Personal Services (Optional)
- Meal plan: $450/month (optional)
- Additional cleaning: $120/month (optional)
- Personal laundry: $80/month (optional)

### 4.3 Utilities
Resident is responsible for:
- Electricity (individual meter)
- Gas (individual meter)  
- Telephone/Internet
- Foxtel/Streaming services

**Estimated utility costs: $180-250/month**

### 4.4 Unit Maintenance
- **Operator pays:** Structural repairs, roof, external painting
- **Resident pays:** Internal repairs, air conditioning, appliances, floor coverings, internal painting

---

## 5. EXIT CONDITIONS

### 5.1 When You Can Leave
You may terminate this contract by giving **90 days written notice** to the Operator.

### 5.2 Exit Payment Timeline
Your exit payment will be calculated and paid within **14 days** of the LATER of:
- Your unit being vacated and returned in good condition, OR
- The unit being resold to a new resident

**IMPORTANT:** You may not receive any money until a new buyer is found for your unit.

### 5.3 Exit Payment Calculation

**Exit Payment = Resale Price - DMF - Selling Costs - Refurbishment Costs**

**Example:**
- Unit resells for: $680,000
- Less DMF (30%): -$195,000
- Less selling costs (3%): -$20,400
- Less refurbishment: -$15,000
- **Your exit payment: $449,600**

### 5.4 Refurbishment Costs
Before resale, the unit must be returned to "as new" condition. Costs may include:
- Repainting (all rooms)
- Carpet replacement
- Kitchen/bathroom repairs
- Garden restoration
- Any damage repair

**Estimated refurbishment: $12,000-$25,000** (deducted from your exit payment)

### 5.5 Marketing and Selling
- The Operator has **exclusive right** to market and sell your unit
- You cannot appoint your own real estate agent
- Selling commission: 3% of sale price (paid by you)
- Marketing period: Typically 3-9 months

---

## 6. CAPITAL GAIN/LOSS

### 6.1 Capital Gain
If the unit resells for MORE than your Ingoing Contribution:
- **You receive: 50% of the capital gain**
- **Operator receives: 50% of the capital gain**

**Example:**
- You paid: $650,000
- Unit resells for: $700,000
- Capital gain: $50,000
- You receive: $25,000 (added to exit payment)
- Operator receives: $25,000

### 6.2 Capital Loss
If the unit resells for LESS than your Ingoing Contribution:
- **You bear: 100% of the capital loss**
- **Operator bears: 0% of the capital loss**

**Example:**
- You paid: $650,000
- Unit resells for: $600,000
- Capital loss: $50,000
- You lose: $50,000 (full amount)

---

## 7. DEATH OF RESIDENT

### 7.1 Contract Continues
Upon death, this contract continues until the unit is vacated and resold.

### 7.2 Estate Obligations
Your estate must:
- Remove all personal belongings within 30 days
- Continue paying General Services Charge until unit is resold
- Pay for any refurbishment required
- Wait for resale before receiving exit payment

### 7.3 Ongoing Charges
**IMPORTANT:** Your estate continues to pay the monthly General Services Charge ($650/month) until the unit is resold, even if vacant.

**Example:** If it takes 6 months to resell, your estate pays $3,900 in ongoing charges.

---

## 8. INHERITANCE IMPACT

### 8.1 What Your Family Receives
Your family will inherit the **Exit Payment** only (not the full Ingoing Contribution).

**Example scenario after 5 years:**
- Original Ingoing: $650,000
- Resale price: $680,000
- Capital gain: $30,000 (you get 50% = $15,000)
- DMF (30%): -$195,000
- Selling costs (3%): -$20,400
- Refurbishment: -$18,000
- **Family receives: ~$461,600**

**Your family LOSES: ~$188,400** from the original $650,000

---

## 9. VILLAGE RULES AND REGULATIONS

### 9.1 Age Restrictions
At least one resident must be **60 years or older** at all times.

### 9.2 Pets
- Small dogs/cats permitted (max 1 pet)
- Must be approved by Village Manager
- Must not disturb other residents
- $500 pet bond required

### 9.3 Renovations
- All renovations require written approval
- Structural changes NOT permitted
- You pay for all approved renovations
- No value added to exit payment for improvements YOU make

### 9.4 Visitors
- Short-term visitors welcome
- Long-term visitors (>6 weeks) require approval
- No subletting permitted

---

## 10. OPERATOR'S OBLIGATIONS

The Operator must:
- Maintain common areas and facilities
- Provide 24/7 emergency call system
- Maintain building insurance
- Comply with Retirement Villages Act
- Provide annual financial statements
- Hold annual residents' meetings

---

## 11. RESIDENT'S OBLIGATIONS

The Resident must:
- Pay all charges on time
- Maintain internal unit condition
- Follow village rules
- Have adequate contents insurance
- Not disturb other residents
- Give 90 days notice before leaving

---

## 12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 12.1 Internal Process
Disputes must first be raised with the Village Manager in writing.

### 12.2 External Resolution
If unresolved, disputes may be referred to:
- State Retirement Village Disputes service
- QCAT (Queensland Civil and Administrative Tribunal)

---

## 13. COOLING-OFF PERIOD

You have **14 days** from signing this contract to cancel without penalty (except reasonable legal/admin fees up to $500).

After 14 days, cancellation is subject to DMF and other exit costs.

---

## 14. FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE

### 14.1 Operator's Financial Position
The Operator must provide annual audited financial statements to all residents.

### 14.2 Village Occupancy
Current occupancy: 87% (156 of 180 units occupied)

### 14.3 Average Resale Time
Average time to resell units: 4-6 months (last 12 months data)

---

## 15. KEY RISKS TO CONSIDER

### ⚠️ Risk 1: Payment Timing
You may wait 6-12 months (or longer) after leaving before receiving your exit payment.

### ⚠️ Risk 2: Market Downturn
If property values fall, you bear 100% of the capital loss.

### ⚠️ Risk 3: High Exit Costs
Total exit costs can be 35-45% of your Ingoing Contribution:
- DMF: 30%
- Selling costs: 3%
- Refurbishment: 2-4%
- Ongoing charges during vacancy: 1-2%

### ⚠️ Risk 4: Estate Burden
Your estate pays ongoing charges until resale (could be $4,000-$8,000 or more).

### ⚠️ Risk 5: No Control Over Sale
You cannot choose your own agent or selling strategy.

---

## 16. IMPORTANT QUESTIONS TO ASK

Before signing, ask the Operator:

1. What was the **average resale time** for units in the last 2 years?
2. What is the **average refurbishment cost** for units like mine?
3. How many units are **currently on the market** unsold?
4. Can you provide **examples of recent exit payments** for similar units?
5. What happens if the Operator **goes bankrupt**?
6. Are there any **planned price increases** for General Services Charge?
7. What **major capital works** are planned (and who pays)?
