═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    FINANCIAL TERMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. ENTRY CONTRIBUTION (INGOING CONTRIBUTION)
   Total Entry Amount: $450,000 (Four Hundred and Fifty Thousand Dollars)
   
   Payment Schedule:
   - Deposit (10%): $45,000 (due upon signing)
   - Balance: $405,000 (due at settlement, within 90 days)

2. DEFERRED MANAGEMENT FEE (DMF)
   The Operator will charge a Deferred Management Fee calculated as follows:
   
   - Year 1: 6% of Entry Contribution = $27,000
   - Year 2: 6% of Entry Contribution = $27,000
   - Year 3: 6% of Entry Contribution = $27,000
   - Year 4: 6% of Entry Contribution = $27,000
   - Year 5+: 6% of Entry Contribution = $27,000
   
   MAXIMUM DMF CAP: 30% of Entry Contribution ($135,000)
   Cap is reached after 5 years of residence.

3. RECURRENT CHARGES (MONTHLY FEES)
   General Service Fee: $780 per month
   
   This fee covers:
   - Building insurance
   - Common area maintenance
   - Gardens and grounds maintenance
   - Security services
   - Emergency call system
   - Administration costs
   
   Note: The General Service Fee is reviewed annually and may increase
   in line with CPI or actual cost increases (whichever is greater).

4. CAPITAL GAINS/LOSSES SHARING
   Upon departure, any capital gain or loss will be shared as follows:
   
   - Resident Share: 50% of capital gain/loss
   - Operator Share: 50% of capital gain/loss
   
   Capital Gain/Loss is calculated as the difference between:
   - The Outgoing Market Value (independently valued), and
   - Your original Entry Contribution ($450,000)

5. EXIT FEE CALCULATION EXAMPLE
   
   Scenario A - After 3 years, property value increases to $500,000:
   
   Outgoing Market Value:                    $500,000
   Less: Entry Contribution:                ($450,000)
   Capital Gain:                              $50,000
   
   Operator's Share of Gain (50%):           ($25,000)
   Deferred Management Fee (3 years @ 6%):  ($81,000)
   
   Total Deductions:                        ($106,000)
   
   ESTIMATED REFUND TO RESIDENT:             $394,000
   
   Scenario B - After 6 years, property value increases to $520,000:
   
   Outgoing Market Value:                    $520,000
   Less: Entry Contribution:                ($450,000)
   Capital Gain:                              $70,000
   
   Operator's Share of Gain (50%):           ($35,000)
   Deferred Management Fee (capped at 30%): ($135,000)
   
   Total Deductions:                        ($170,000)
   
   ESTIMATED REFUND TO RESIDENT:             $350,000

6. RENOVATION/REFURBISHMENT COSTS
   Upon departure, the Operator may deduct reasonable costs for:
   - Returning the unit to original condition (fair wear and tear excluded)
   - Necessary repairs beyond normal wear and tear
   - Carpet steam cleaning and professional cleaning
   
   Maximum deduction: $15,000 (unless damage exceeds normal wear)

                    TERMS AND CONDITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7. RESIDENCE RIGHTS
   7.1 You have the right to occupy the Unit for life, subject to:
       - Payment of all fees when due
       - Compliance with Village Rules
       - Not causing nuisance to other residents
   
   7.2 This is a LICENCE TO OCCUPY (not ownership or lease)
   
   7.3 You cannot sublease or assign your residence rights

8. COOLING OFF PERIOD
   You have 21 days from signing this contract to withdraw without penalty.
   Full refund of deposit will be provided if you withdraw during this period.

9. TERMINATION OF CONTRACT
   This contract terminates when:
   9.1 You provide 90 days written notice of intention to leave
   9.2 You pass away
   9.3 You permanently move to higher care facility
   9.4 Serious breach of Village Rules (after warnings)

10. REFUND PAYMENT TIMELINE
    The Operator will pay your refund within 14 days of the LATER of:
    - Re-sale of the unit to a new resident
    - Settlement of the new resident's entry contribution
    - 12 months from the date you vacate
    
    MAXIMUM WAIT TIME: 12 months (unless unit has not been re-sold)

11. MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
    
    OPERATOR RESPONSIBLE FOR:
    - External walls, roof, foundations
    - Common areas
    - Essential services (water, electricity, gas infrastructure)
    - Building insurance
    
    RESIDENT RESPONSIBLE FOR:
    - Internal fixtures and fittings
    - Carpet and floor coverings
    - Kitchen appliances
    - Interior painting and decorating
    - Air conditioning units (if installed by resident)
    - Contents insurance

12. AGED CARE ARRANGEMENTS
    12.1 If you require aged care, you may need to move to appropriate facility
    12.2 The village has a priority transfer arrangement with "Caring Hearts Aged Care"
    12.3 You may remain in the unit if care can be adequately provided
    12.4 You continue to pay monthly fees until contract termination

13. VILLAGE RULES
    Key rules include:
    - Pets: Small pets permitted with approval (under 10kg)
    - Visitors: Overnight guests limited to 30 days per year
    - Age requirement: At least one resident must be 55+ years
    - Noise: Quiet hours 10pm-7am
    - Parking: One vehicle per unit in designated space
    
    Full Village Rules document provided separately.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
    Any disputes must first be addressed through:
    - Discussion with Village Manager
    - Mediation (if discussion unsuccessful)
    - Victorian Civil and Administrative Tribunal (VCAT) as last resort

15. COOLING OFF AND LEGAL ADVICE
    ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTICES:
    
    - You MUST seek independent legal advice before signing
    - You have 21 days cooling off period (from date of signing)
    - The Operator recommends engaging a solicitor experienced in 
      retirement village contracts
    - Government information available at: consumer.vic.gov.au

                    IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RED FLAGS TO DISCUSS WITH YOUR LAWYER:

⚠️ DEFERRED MANAGEMENT FEE: You will lose $135,000 (30% of entry price)
   if you stay 5 years or longer. This is NOT refundable.

⚠️ CAPITAL GAINS SHARING: You only receive 50% of any increase in 
   property value. The operator takes the other 50%.

⚠️ MONTHLY FEE INCREASES: Fees can increase annually and there is no 
   cap on increases (typically CPI or cost increases).

⚠️ REFUND TIMING: You may wait up to 12 months for your refund after 
   leaving, as payment depends on resale of your unit.

⚠️ NO OWNERSHIP: You do not own the unit. This is a licence to occupy.
   You cannot leave it to your children in your will.
